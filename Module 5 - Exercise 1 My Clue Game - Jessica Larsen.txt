//Planning for Clue Game
divide cards into three stacks: suspects, weapons, and rooms
shuffle the three piles seperately
take the top card from each pile without looking and place it in the Confidential Case File
Now shuffle the remaining cards together thoroughly
now divide the remaining cards evenly into two piles of 9
One pile is the player's(me), one pile is the computer's

player/computer rolls dice
if player/computer enough to move to the room they want to
	they move to that room, and continue with their turn and guessing
else they move a little bit and their turn ends

player makes a guess of who committed the crime, guessing one suspect, weapon and room (e.g. I think it was Scarlet in the Garage with the Candlestick)
if computer has the a card(s) that matches one of those guessed
	computer shows one of the cards to player
else 
	computer communicates that it does not have any of those cards

computer makes a guess of who committed the crime, guessing one suspect, weapon and room
if player has the a card(s) that matches one of those guessed
	player shows one of the cards to player
else 
	player communicates that it does not have any of those cards

as gameplay continues player and computer make guesses based on what they have already
it turns into a battle of not only luck but also strategy and wits as they try to bluff with one another and show repeat cards to the other as much as they can

eventually either player or computer thinks they know what cards are in the Confidential Case File
Player or computer will then guess out loud what cards they think are in there
if player or computer is correct
	they win
else 
	they loose and the other one wins
